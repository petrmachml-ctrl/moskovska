## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS and browser ES modules. Node 24 provides development and build scripts. This is an implementation choice for the requested lightweight public preview, not a framework preference stated by the user. Hosting target: GitHub Pages in the selected public repository.

## Users
People considering housing or business premises in central Martin; the investor reviewing a proposed sales website.

## Product Purpose
Translate the user-selected variant A into a working, responsive, publicly accessible preview. Visitors can explore the eight demonstration units, open a shareable unit detail, inspect illustrations and try an explicitly non-sending inquiry form.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The supplied brief describes four flats, two administrative premises, one retail/service unit and one attic apartment, plus seven planned parking spaces. Investor: BALCON, s. r. o. Building works are planned to end on 30 October 2027; completion approval and handover dates are not confirmed.

Dom BALCON is a working name. The user explicitly authorized fictional photographs and unit data, visibly marked as illustration. All IDs, areas, configurations and availability are synthetic. Office premises and the attic apartment must not be relabelled as flats. Interior, balcony, terrace and storage areas remain separate. Prices are on request in this preview.

Actual plans, contact details, final specification and a real inquiry destination have not been supplied. The preview must not invent reachable contacts, imply an inquiry was sent, or collect and persist personal data. No analytics, advertising tags, cookies or third-party embeds are installed. Preview indexing is discouraged with robots metadata and robots.txt.

## Brand Commitments
User selected variant A, Mestská architektúra, and requested a prominent Náhled label. The approved illustrated reference is recorded in `.impeccable/surfaces/home.md`. Website language is Slovak; Náhled is retained as requested.

## Evidence on Hand
